# Roomie Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Roomie is a shared-home app for groceries, recipes, home info and shared expenses. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it and how to delete it.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-in (one-time code) and account contact |
| Apple ID identifier, and the email Apple shares (possibly a private relay address) | Sign in with Apple |
| Name, surname, phone number, date of birth, profile photo | Your profile, shown to the people in your room |
| Groceries, recipes, home info, expenses and your share of them | The app's core features, shared with your room or kept private if you choose |
| Photos you attach (profile, recipes, home info) | Shown in the app to you and your room |
| Push notification token | Notifying you about new groceries and shared expenses (you can turn this off in Profile) |
| Crash and error reports | Finding and fixing bugs |

We don't sell your data, show ads or use third-party analytics or tracking. We don't use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

## Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract). We process crash reports under our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. We send push notifications with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed immediately from our live systems, and from backups within 30 days.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from **Profile → Delete account**. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

What is deleted:

- Your profile: name, surname, email, phone, date of birth and photo.
- Your sign-in, so signing in again with the same email starts a new account.
- Your private grocery and expense lists, and everything in them.
- Your share of every expense, and the "who it's for" tags on grocery items.
- Room invites you created.
- The room itself, with all its lists, recipes, expenses, home info and photos, if you were its last member.

What stays with the room for the people still in it:

- Shared groceries, recipes, home info, and the photos attached to them.
- Shared expenses. An expense doesn't store who added it, so nothing on it names you after your share is removed.

Data on your phone is wiped when the deletion succeeds. If you can't access the app, email us and we'll delete your account for you.

Roomie is not intended for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
